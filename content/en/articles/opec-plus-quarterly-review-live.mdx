## How we are covering this

Entries marked as a **market move** describe price behaviour, not a decision. Entries without a label describe something that was announced or confirmed. We keep the two visually distinct because during a live session they arrive interleaved, and a price move reported in the same style as a decision is how a rumour becomes a fact.

Where an early report is later corrected, the correction appears as its own entry rather than as a silent edit to an earlier one.
